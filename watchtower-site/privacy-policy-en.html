LEO GAMES STUDIO / WATCHTOWER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This notice describes processing on the studio’s public pages, Watchtower and the $WTWR information page. Games and third-party services have their own notices.

1. Scope and responsibility

The public pages are provided under the Leo Games Studio and Watchtower names. In this notice, “we” and “the project” refer to the team maintaining those pages and their interest forms.

This notice covers the studio website, the $WTWR information page and interest forms shown on those pages. It does not describe account data or gameplay telemetry inside ARES-1, GUTTERCAPS, NeuroForge, Neon Relay, wallets, blockchains, apps, the Watchtower operator dashboard or external platforms. Those services may have separate operators and notices.

2. What data is processed

Forms and referral events

The interest forms currently have no configured submission endpoint. The form code checks the email format but does not send the address to the project or store it in project systems. It remains in the browser field until cleared or the page is closed; the interface says that the form is not connected.

The referral-event endpoint is also not configured, so no event is sent. If an incoming link contains a valid `wt_click` parameter, the script may remove it from the address bar after the page loads. The initial request containing that parameter may already have been sent to the web host and included in its logs.

If forms or attribution are enabled, this notice will be updated before collection begins to describe recipients, purposes, legal bases, retention and available user choices. The current first-party code does not use cookies for forms or advertising analytics.

Technical requests and logs

When a page is opened, the browser connects to the hosting platform. Depending on the platform and its configuration, requests or logs may include an IP address, date and time, requested path and query parameters, browser and device details, language, referrer and response status. Retention is determined by the live platform’s settings and terms.

Google Fonts

The Website loads font stylesheets and files from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. The browser connects directly to Google’s infrastructure; Google may receive the IP address and technical request data. Google’s terms and Privacy Policy apply to that interaction.

Third-party sites

When a user follows a link to a game or other external resource, that operator may process technical, account, gameplay, payment or wallet data under its own rules.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Technical request data may be processed to deliver pages, maintain operation, diagnose errors and protect the Website from abuse. Where a legal basis must be identified, it is assessed under applicable law and the actual hosting configuration.

Interest forms and referral-event transfers are currently disabled: the project does not receive email addresses entered in those forms or referral events. Before enabling either feature, its purpose, recipient, legal basis and retention period will be defined; consent will be requested separately where required by law.

4. Recipients and international processing

Technical data may be processed by the hosting platform and Google Fonts. Their infrastructure may be located in another country. The specific hosting platform and its retention periods depend on the live deployment; third parties also process data under their own terms.

Transfers outside a user’s country may be governed by data-protection laws applicable to the relevant provider and request. If a future integration sends user data to the project, information about its recipients and required safeguards will be added before launch.

5. Retention and security

The current form code does not send or store email addresses in project systems. Retention of technical logs depends on the hosting configuration. If forms, analytics or support are introduced, retention periods, deletion procedures and access controls will be defined for each data category.

We aim to apply reasonable safeguards to the Website and data handled by its code. Internet transmission and storage on technical platforms cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. User rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have the right to request access to personal data, correction or deletion, restriction of processing, a portable copy, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before the request. Users may also complain to a competent data-protection supervisory authority.

The Website does not currently provide a dedicated form or published channel for privacy requests. This does not waive rights granted by applicable law.

7. Children and minors

The Website is not specifically directed to children and does not currently connect an email-collection form. Before processing minors’ personal data, applicable age requirements, parental or guardian authorisation and other rules in the relevant jurisdictions must be considered.

8. Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated if the hosting, forms, analytics, fonts or other data flows change. The last-updated date appears at the top of the page; material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
