How the branches connect

🥔→🌾 Potatoes as seeds

$POTATO from ARES-1 is planting material in Age of Farming. AOF harvest upgrades ARES-1 fields. Game tokens get spent in a neighbouring game, not just sold.

🧢 Trophy caps

A Neon Relay race win or an Epic ARES-1 module unlocks a unique GutterCaps cap. Holding an NFT in one game gives a bonus in another.

🪪 Watchtower Passport

A non-transferable NFT that accumulates achievements across all games. Passport level raises the staking multiplier and gives early access to new games.

🔁 Buyback from revenue

A share of game and platform fees buys $WTWR on the market: half is burned, half goes to stakers. Buybacks happen only from actual revenue.

FAQ

How is $WTWR different from $POTATO and $CG?

Game tokens run the economy of a specific game: farming, crafting, bets. $WTWR is the platform layer: service access, staking, Launchpad, buybacks from the whole ecosystem's revenue.

Why does SKR weigh less in staking?

So the airdrop goes primarily to ecosystem players, not to outside capital that came only for the giveaway.

Who controls the money?

The treasury is a multisig; spending is published quarterly. Compute Grid equipment is in a public inventory with receipts.

What if the target is not reached?

Round 0 (NFT) and round 1 fund the audit and mainnet for two games. The minimum and refund rules are published before the round starts.
